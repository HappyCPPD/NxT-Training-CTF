Reveal the writeup (contains the flag)

The site encrypts with XOR and uses the same key for everything. XOR undoes itself, so:

  • secret ⊕ key = C1 and yours ⊕ key = C2
  • so C1 ⊕ C2 ⊕ yours = secret

Encrypt a long string of As. In CyberChef, take the secret's hex, apply From Hex, then XOR with your encrypted hex (key type HEX), then XOR with AAAA… (key type UTF8). Out comes your personal flag.

Takeaway: never reuse a one-time key. This exact mistake broke Soviet spy traffic in the 1940s (Project VENONA) and Wi-Fi encryption in the 2000s (WEP).
