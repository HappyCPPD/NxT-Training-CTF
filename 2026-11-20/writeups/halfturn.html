"Applying it twice gives you the original" is the definition of ROT13: shift each letter 13 places, and since 13 + 13 = 26, doing it twice gets you back where you started. CyberChef → ROT13 → NxT{twice_is_nice}.

Takeaway: a cipher with no key is not a cipher. It's a puzzle with one answer.
