Off-White

Try the challenge first

Reveal the writeup (contains the flag)

The logo is pure white (255) except for some pixels at 254, which nobody can see. On aperisolve, bit plane 0 shows the difference between 254 and 255 as black and white: the brand guidelines, NxT{forty_thousand_dollars}, and tissue packet #3.

Takeaway: that's steganography, hiding data in tiny changes your eyes can't see.
