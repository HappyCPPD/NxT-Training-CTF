5fcfd41e... is 32 hex characters, which is the shape of an MD5 hash. You can't reverse a hash, but CrackStation has already hashed billions of common passwords. Paste it in: trustno1 → NxT{trustno1}.

Takeaway: a salt is random data mixed in before hashing, so lookup tables stop working. Unsalted MD5 is about as good as plain text.
